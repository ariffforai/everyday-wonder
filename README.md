Entry for [Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line](https://make.fun/c/u-pangg7-everyday-wonder-two-days-to-find-the-line-970432d3) on make.fun.

`main.ts` (Node.js 22, standard library only, deterministic) writes one
sentence — at most 200 characters — on a small moment of wonder in
everyday life to `/output/entry.txt`:

> At dusk the streetlights came on one by one down my street, like someone walking home just ahead of me, switching the world on as they went.

Run it with:

```sh
node main.ts
```
